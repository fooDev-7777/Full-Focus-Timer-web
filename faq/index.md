## <a id="faq-shield"></a>1. Focus Shield (overlay) is not working, or I want to deactivate it immediately

### How to enable the Focus Shield

The Focus Shield relies on Android's **"Display over other apps"** permission (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`) to block distracting apps while your focus timer is running.

1. Open **Full Focus Timer** → open the menu (☰) → go to **Settings**.
2. Toggle on **"Focus Shield (Block Other Apps)"** and follow the prompt to grant **"Display over other apps"** permission.
3. Alternatively, open your Android device's **Settings → Apps → Full Focus Timer → Display over other apps** and enable **Allow display over other apps**.

> **Note:** Even if you choose not to grant this permission, you can still use Full Focus Timer as a standard Pomodoro and ambient-sound timer without the Focus Shield.

### How to deactivate the Focus Shield immediately

You can turn off the Focus Shield at any time:

- Tap **"Give Up"** on the timer screen, or stop the timer. The overlay is removed immediately and normal access to all apps is restored.

### Emergency calls and alerts

Incoming phone calls, alarms, and public emergency alerts are handled in accordance with Android OS specifications. However, depending on your device model, OS version, or settings, the overlay may make alerts harder to notice or slightly delay screen interaction. **In an emergency, tap "Give Up" or stop the timer immediately** to dismiss the shield.

> **Privacy note:** The "Display over other apps" permission is used strictly to display the Focus Shield while your timer is running. The App never reads, captures, or monitors screen contents or user inputs from other apps.

## <a id="faq-notifications"></a>2. Timer or daily reminder notifications do not arrive, or the timer stops in the background

Modern Android devices apply aggressive battery-saving restrictions that can pause background timers or delay scheduled reminders. Please check the following settings on your device:

1. **Exempt Full Focus Timer from Battery Optimization (Most Important):**
   - Go to Android **Settings → Apps → Full Focus Timer → Battery** (or **App battery usage**) and select **"Unrestricted"** (or **"Don't optimize"**).
2. **Allow Notifications (Android 13 and higher):**
   - Go to Android **Settings → Apps → Full Focus Timer → Notifications** and make sure **All Full Focus Timer notifications** are enabled.
3. **Allow Alarms & Reminders (Android 12 and higher):**
   - Go to Android **Settings → Apps → Full Focus Timer → Alarms & reminders** and enable **"Allow setting alarms and reminders"** so daily reminders fire at your exact scheduled time.
4. **Manufacturer-Specific Power-Saving Settings:**
   - **Samsung (One UI):** Open **Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Never auto sleeping apps** and add **Full Focus Timer**.
   - **Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (MIUI / HyperOS):** Open **Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Full Focus Timer** → set **Battery saver** to **"No restrictions"** and enable **Autostart**.
   - **OPPO / Realme / OnePlus:** Open **Settings → Apps → App management → Full Focus Timer → Battery usage** → enable **"Allow background activity"** and **"Allow auto-launch"**.
   - **Huawei:** Open **Settings → Battery → App launch** → disable automatic management for **Full Focus Timer** and enable **Auto-launch**, **Secondary launch**, and **Run in background**.

> **Tip:** Daily reminders are automatically rescheduled after your device restarts, provided auto-launch/background execution is not blocked by your device's power-saving software.

## <a id="faq-restore"></a>4. How do I restore my Premium purchase after switching devices or reinstalling?

Your Premium purchase is tied to your **Google Play account**, so you do not need to buy it again when you reinstall the App or switch to a new Android device.

### Steps to restore

1. Sign in to the **Google Play Store** app on your device using the **same Google account** you used to buy Premium.
2. Open **Full Focus Timer**.
3. Go to **Settings** (or open the **Premium** screen from the menu) and tap **"Restore Purchases"**.
4. Once Google Play verifies your purchase, Premium features will be unlocked immediately.

### If restoration does not work right away

- **Multiple Google accounts:** If your device has more than one Google account, make sure the account that purchased Premium is selected as the active account in the Google Play Store app, then reinstall Full Focus Timer from that account if necessary.
- **Check your purchase history:** Open Google Play Store → tap your profile icon → **Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history** and verify that the Full Focus Timer Premium purchase is listed.
- **Network & restart:** Ensure you have an active internet connection, close the App completely, reopen it, and tap **"Restore Purchases"** again.
- Still having trouble? [Contact support](#faq-contact) with your Google Play Order ID (format: `GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx`).

## <a id="faq-ads-privacy"></a>7. How do I manage ads, privacy consent, and crash reporting?

Full Focus Timer respects your privacy and complies with GDPR / UK GDPR and Google Play privacy requirements.

### Updating or withdrawing consent in the App

In regions where consent management is required (such as the **EEA, the UK, and Switzerland**), the App uses **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to obtain your consent preferences.

- You can review, change, or withdraw your consent at any time by going to **Settings → Privacy Settings** inside the App.

### Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) linked to consent

- **Disabled by default at launch:** Firebase Crashlytics is disabled when the App starts.
- **Consent-gated activation:** Crash log collection is enabled **only** when consent eligibility (`canRequestAds`) is granted via Google UMP.
- **Instant opt-out on withdrawal:** If you withdraw your consent via **Settings → Privacy Settings**, Firebase Crashlytics crash data collection is stopped accordingly.

### Additional ad controls on Android

- On your Android device, open **Settings → Google → Ads** to **Delete advertising ID** or **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.
- You can also manage Google ad preferences at [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

For full details on data handling, third-party SDKs, and your privacy rights, please read our [Privacy Policy](../#privacy-policy).

## <a id="faq-backup"></a>8. How do data backup and device migration work?

Full Focus Timer uses a **local-first architecture** — there is no user account registration and no developer backend server storing your data.

### What is automatically backed up (Android Auto Backup)

If **Android Auto Backup** is enabled on your device (typically **Settings → Google → Backup**), Android automatically backs up the following data in encrypted form to your personal Google account and restores it when you set up a new device:

- **Included in backup:** Timer presets, focus session history (statistics), and daily reminder notification settings.
- **Excluded from backup:** Onboarding state, permission prompts, accepted Terms version, Force Shield ON/OFF setting, local Premium cache, and advertising consent preferences.

### Moving to a new Android device

1. Make sure **Android Backup** is enabled on your old device and has completed a recent backup (**Settings → Google → Backup → Back up now**).
2. Set up your new Android device with the **same Google account** and restore your apps & data during setup.
3. Open Full Focus Timer on your new device:
   - Your presets, focus history, and reminder settings will be restored automatically via Android Auto Backup.
   - If you purchased Premium, go to **Settings** (or the **Premium** screen) and tap **"Restore Purchases"** to restore your Premium status from Google Play.
   - Re-grant **"Display over other apps"**, **"Notifications"**, and **"Alarms & reminders"** permissions as needed on the new device.

For more details, see [Privacy Policy — Section 3 (Android Auto Backup)](../#pp-auto-backup).
