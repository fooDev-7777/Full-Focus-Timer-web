The following data is stored exclusively on your device using Android local storage (Room database and Preferences DataStore) to provide the App's core features:

- **Timer Presets:** Preset names, work durations, break durations, number of cycles, and selected ambient sound identifiers (for work and break sessions).

- **Reminder Notification Settings:** Whether reminders are enabled (ON/OFF), scheduled reminder time, and custom reminder message text.
- **App Preferences & Consent State:** Force Shield setting (ON/OFF), onboarding completion status, permission prompt status, accepted Terms version, and premium status.

**No Direct Personal Information Collected:** The App does not require user registration and does not ask for or collect directly identifiable personal information such as your name, home address, phone number, or email address (unless you voluntarily email us for support).
